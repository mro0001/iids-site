Working with IIDS

What do you need?

The Institute for Interdisciplinary Data Sciences runs the university's research-computing and genomics cores — and helps researchers win and manage the awards that use them. Everything we do fits behind one of these four doors.

Pick your need

Computing and data

High performance computing, storage, hosting, scientific programming, and help managing research data — through Research Computing and Data Services (RCDS). Most of it at no direct charge; first step is an account.

Genomics and bioinformatics

Sequencing, library preparation, and bioinformatics — through the Genomics and Bioinformatics Resources Core (GBRC). Orders run through iLab; external researchers welcome.

Submitting a proposal

Three paths — which one is you?

Using IIDS, RCDS, or GBRC resources — you submit through your department or college, but the work will use institute computing, genomics, or people. Declare IIDS affiliation when you submit — it cannot be added later.
