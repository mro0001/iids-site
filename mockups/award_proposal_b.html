Who does what

The source is a responsibility list for three parties. This is that list.

You / the traveler

The principal investigator

  • Contact IIDS for any IIDS responsibility or question.
  • Get IIDS preapproval before deviating from the budgeted primary expense spending categories.
  • Assign Jaggaer carts using IIDS indices to Dalynne Veeder.
  • Request travel authorization in advance of travel using the IIDS forms.
  • Add both Mollyann Jones and Dalynne Veeder as Chrome River delegates.

Your home department

  • Conducts all hiring searches for benefit-eligible employees — after confirming availability of funding with the IIDS office.
  • Issues graduate offer letters; processes graduate appointments and re-appointments.
  • Processes student tuition and fee payments, including Mollyann Jones in the approval queue.
  • Processes all appointment and re-appointment EPAFs.
  • Includes both Mollyann Jones and Dalynne Veeder as FYI on every EPAF affecting org codes 897 and 936.

IIDS

The money

  • Processes all POs, requisitions, requests for bids, sourcing requests, and service contracts.
  • Processes purchasing card expenses 100% on IIDS managed indices.
  • Processes travel pre-authorizations and claims on IIDS managed indices.
  • Processes labor redistributions, home department as FYI.
  • Reviews and approves summer salary on org codes 897 and 936.
The compliance rules that govern all of it
  • Grant expenditures must follow University of Idaho and State of Idaho guidelines.
  • Grant expenditures must follow federal guidelines, including being allowable, allocable and reasonable (2 CFR 200 Subpart E).
  • On mixed transactions the index incurring the majority of the expense processes it, with the other involved units listed as approvers or FYI.
  • If your home department processes expenses in Chrome River on an IIDS managed index (org 897 and 936), Mollyann Jones must be included in those approvals.

Travel

The only domain here with a sequence.

  • 1

    Once, before your first trip

    Every traveler should add both Mollyann Jones and Dalynne Veeder as Chrome River delegates.

  • 2

    Request authorization — in advance

    The traveler must request travel authorization before travelling, using the IIDS form. IIDS then creates a pre-approval request in Chrome River and emails the traveler and the approvers when it is ready.

  • 3

    Wait for every approver

    Filing the form is not approval. Travel is approved only when the traveler and all other approvers have approved the Chrome River pre-approval report. Reference: UI APM 70.

  • 4

    Claim afterwards

    Employees requesting reimbursement, or U of I P-card expenses processed, should do so through a travel claim or a non-travel claim.

Taking personal days? Do not use a UI purchasing card to book airfare or pay for lodging — use your own funds and request personal reimbursement. Provide comparable itineraries and costs for travel without the personal days.

International travel must be registered and approved in the International Travel Registry and must comply with the Fly America Act.

What the form asks for, and per diem

Traveler name, V number, supervisor, department, trip title, destination, depart and return dates and times; estimated airfare, lodging, personal vehicle miles, ground transportation and miscellaneous; per diem; U of I index number(s) and college; description and justification; whether personal leave is planned. Attachments: pdf, jpg, jpeg, png, doc, docx.

Per diem references: U of I · GSA

Non-employee travellers

Chrome River transactions apply to U of I employees only. Non-employee travel bypasses the traveler approval process.

Travel questions: Dalynne Veeder, (208) 885-9076.

Submitting another proposal

A funded PI is usually a repeat submitter. The full policy lives on its own page.

4 weeks before the sponsor's deadline if the proposal is collaborative or includes subawards or contracts. 3 weeks otherwise. It is a request Mollyann Jones takes to the Director, who evaluates it — not a form that is simply processed.

What your request email must contain
  • The RFA (a PDF or link) for the program
  • The submission deadline
  • A list of the PIs and Co-PIs, including external collaborators
  • A brief summary of the project, 2 to 5 sentences
  • A brief justification, 2 to 5 sentences, explaining why submission through IIDS is appropriate

What IIDS does behind the scenes

Post-award support functions.

The money

Expenditure tracking across salaries, purchases and travel; early budget setup and advance funding requests; subaward initiation and monitoring; sponsor pre-approvals for no-cost extensions, re-budgets, expenditure reviews and compliance; budget and payroll cost transfers; proactive comprehensive financial reports and projections; timely and compliant closeouts.

Communicating the research

Art and graphic design for figures and artwork for websites, manuscripts and presentations; animated and interactive online visualizations; photography and video supporting dissemination of faculty and student research; press releases and social media content.

Large programs

Programmatic coordination of large program grants such as training grants, COBRE, and NSF EPSCOR Track II awards.
