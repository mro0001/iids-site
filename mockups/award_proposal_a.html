    Travel

    Once, before your first trip: every traveler should add both Mollyann Jones and Dalynne Veeder as Chrome River delegates.

    Traveler requests · IIDS builds the pre-approval · Traveler + approvers approve

    The traveler must request travel authorization in advance of travel using the IIDS form. IIDS then creates a pre-approval request in Chrome River and emails the traveler and approvers when it is ready.

    Filing the form is not approval. Travel is approved only when the traveler and all other approvers have approved the Chrome River pre-approval report (UI APM 70).

    Taking personal days? Do not use a UI purchasing card to book airfare or pay for lodging — use your own funds and request personal reimbursement. Provide comparable itineraries and costs for travel without the personal days.

    International travel must be registered and approved in the International Travel Registry and must comply with the Fly America Act.

    What the travel form asks for

    Traveler name, V number, supervisor, department, trip title, destination, depart and return dates and times; estimated airfare, lodging, personal vehicle miles, ground transportation, and miscellaneous; per diem; U of I index number(s) and college; description and justification of the trip; whether personal leave is planned. Attachments accepted: pdf, jpg, jpeg, png, doc, docx.

    Per diem references: U of I · GSA

    Non-employee travelers

    Chrome River transactions apply to U of I employees only. Non-employee travel bypasses the traveler approval process.

    Travel questions: Dalynne Veeder, (208) 885-9076.

    Submit your next proposal through IIDS

    Submission through IIDS is a request you make to Mollyann Jones — the IIDS Director and relevant staff evaluate it.

    4 weeks before the sponsor's deadline if the proposal is collaborative or includes subawards or contracts. 3 weeks otherwise.

    What your request email must contain
    • The RFA (a PDF or link) for the program
    • The submission deadline
    • A list of the PIs and Co-PIs, including external collaborators
    • A brief summary of the project, 2 to 5 sentences
    • A brief justification, 2 to 5 sentences, explaining why submission through IIDS is appropriate
