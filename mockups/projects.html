Research enabled by IIDS

Built from the University of Idaho sponsored-projects record, filtered to funded awards affiliated with IIDS — proposals that were declined, withdrawn or are still pending are not listed. Every description here traces to a public source: where a funder publishes an abstract it is quoted verbatim and linked, and where one does not, the description comes from the project's own site and says so. Records marked low confidence are confirmed awards whose sponsor publishes no description at all. Coverage is a floor rather than a census — it reflects the proposals in that record, not everything IIDS has touched.
