New to IIDS?

The Institute for Interdisciplinary Data Sciences (IIDS) runs the university's research-computing and genomics cores and helps researchers win and manage the awards that use them. Never worked with the institute before? One of the doors below is almost certainly yours — and if none of them fit, talk to a person.

Computing and data

High performance computing, storage, hosting, scientific programming, and data services, through Research Computing and Data Services (RCDS).

First step: request a research-computing account — one per person; include your PI's name if you are not faculty.

What does it cost?

Less than you expect. U of I researchers receive a set of core services at no direct charge — accounts, storage to the group allowance, consultation, and hosting — and genomics work runs at subsidized internal rates through iLab. The subsidy carries one obligation in return: affiliate the sponsored work that uses IIDS with the institute when you submit it — it cannot be added later.

Frequently asked questions

Who can use IIDS services?

IIDS services are available to all U of I researchers; graduate students and postdocs come in through their PI's research group. External researchers are welcome at GBRC through iLab, at the full-cost rates federal service-center rules require.

Policy: Subsidized Services → · Internal and external users: who pays which rate →

How can the services be subsidized?

Through a simplified facilities and administrative (F&A) model: when researchers affiliate their sponsored projects with IIDS, a share of the F&A those awards return helps sustain the cores, alongside university investment. That is what lets the institute subsidize core services rather than charging faculty for them — and it is why declaring your affiliation accurately matters.

Policy: Facilities and Administrative (F&A) Split → · Policy: Subsidized Services →

How do I indicate my affiliation with IIDS on a proposal?

Check the box at submission — it cannot be added later. If your sponsored work will use IIDS infrastructure, personnel, or resources, check the IIDS affiliation box in VERAS when the proposal routes. The walkthrough shows exactly where the box is, and the Proposals page carries the full rules.

Indicating IIDS affiliation in VERAS → · Proposals — Declare it →

Does U of I have AI tools I can use?

Yes. MindRouter gives students, staff, and faculty campus-hosted large language models — a university login and a self-serve API key away — and the Vandalizer brings AI document intelligence to research administration. The Accessing AI guide collects every option and how to get access to each.
